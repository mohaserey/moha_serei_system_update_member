Mohaserei System Update - Member Directory (MSN)"មនុស្សគ្រប់រូបមានតម្លៃសេដ្ឋកិច្ច។ រាល់ការចូលរួមមានតម្លៃ។"Every individual has economic value. Every participation counts.🌟 Overview / សេចក្តីផ្តើមThe Mohaserei Member Directory (moha_serei_system_update_member.html) is the core Master Data Registry module (MDR-001) of the Mohaserei Network (MSN) ecosystem. It provides real-time visibility, filtering, and governance oversight for network members across all 25 capitals and provinces of Cambodia.Through this module, administrators and coordinators can search members by regional hierarchy, track active vs. inactive participation, evaluate Universal Economic Scores (UES), and inspect individual Digital Economic Passports (ECO-002).🏛️ Core System Modules Integrated / ម៉ូឌុលស្នូលក្នុងប្រព័ន្ធMDR-001 (Master Member Registry): Centralized tracking of member identities (MSN-KH-XXXX), contact details, and regional assignments.ECO-002 (Digital Economic Passport): Interactive profile modal displaying a member's Economic ID, Economic Rank, Local Governance Level, Universal Economic Score (UES), Human Capital Value (HCV), and Network Size.GOV-008 (Local Governance Hierarchy): Automatically maps member leadership titles from Village Leaders up to Provincial and National Leaders based on active network thresholds.ECO-001 & RANK-001 (UES & Leadership Ranking): Displays real-time score accumulation and automated rank progression (Member $\rightarrow$ Associate $\rightarrow$ Coordinator $\rightarrow$ Supervisor $\rightarrow$ Manager $\rightarrow$ Director).👥 5 Participation Categories / ប្រភេទចូលរួមទាំង ៥ ក្នុងបណ្ដាញThe directory categorizes and filters members across five core economic participation streams:១. អ្នកមានកម្លាំងពលកម្ម + ពេលវេលា (Labor & Time Capital): Active and inactive agents/members engaged in direct sales and logistics.២. អ្នកមានចំណេះជំនាញ (Knowledge & Skill Capital): Professionals in accounting, training/coaching, IT/digital technology, and technical fields.៣. អ្នកមានធនធាន (Resource & Financial Capital): Contributors providing assets, liquid capital, and investment funds.៤. អ្នកមានបណ្ដាញទំនាក់ទំនង (Network Capital): Human resource connectors and business network builders.៥. អ្នកម្ចាស់អាជីវកម្ម (Business Capital): Shop owners, handicrafts, community enterprises, SMEs, and factories.🔍 Key Features / មុខងារសំខាន់ៗMulti-Criteria Filtering: Filter members instantaneously by:Name or MSN Member ID (MSN-KH-XXXX)Capital / Province (All 25 regions of Cambodia)Participation Category (Categories 1 through 5)Activity Status (សកម្ម (Active) vs. អសកម្ម (Inactive))Dynamic Summary Metrics: Automatically recalculates displayed member counts, active member totals, and cumulative Universal Economic Scores (UES) based on active filters.Digital Economic Passport Modal (ECO-002): One-click inspection of a member's complete economic footprint, Human Capital Value (HCV), and governance role.Seamless Ecosystem Navigation: Direct links to the National Dashboard and Member Registration portals.🛠️ Technology Stack / បច្ចេកវិទ្យាប្រើប្រាស់Frontend: HTML5, CSS3 (Custom CSS Variables & Responsive Grid/Flexbox), Vanilla JavaScript (ES6+)Typography & Icons: Google Fonts (Kantumruy Pro) & FontAwesome 6.4.0Branding: Official Mohaserei Network logo asset (mohaserey.jpg)📂 🚀 Usage / របៀបប្រើប្រាស់Ensure mohaserey.jpg and the companion HTML files (moha_serei_system_update_dashboard.html and moha_serei_system_update_registration.html) are placed in the same root directory.Open moha_serei_system_update_member.html in any modern web browser.Use the top filter bar to narrow down members by province, participation category, or status, and click "មើលព័ត៌មាន" (View Details) on any row to inspect their Digital Economic Passport.📄 Governance & Compliance / អភិបាលកិច្ចOperates in full compliance with the MSN Master Charter (MSN-CHARTER-001), Economic Constitution (MSN-CONSTITUTION-001), and Master Data Governance Framework (MSN-DATA-001) of the Mohaserei Network.
